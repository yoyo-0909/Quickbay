# QuickBay visual identity

QuickBay should look precise, dependable and contemporary. The visual system is inspired by workshop geometry, route planning and well-organised service operations.

## Colour palette

| Name | Hex | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Ink | #080B10 | Primary dark surfaces |
| Slate | #17242B | Secondary dark surfaces |
| Signal lime | #D8F36A | Accent and important highlights |
| Paper | #F7FAFC | Light text and surfaces |
| Steel | #9AAAB2 | Supporting text and borders |

## Typography

- Use a clean sans-serif family such as Inter, Plus Jakarta Sans or Arial.
- Keep headings short, strong and sentence-case.
- Use generous spacing and clear hierarchy.
- Avoid novelty display fonts and excessive all-caps copy.

## Graphic language

- Prefer geometric shapes, workshop-grid motifs, route lines and clear process diagrams.
- Use the QuickBay Q mark as a standalone brand asset.
- Use assets/quickbay-banner.svg at the top of the GitHub README.
- Keep illustrations functional; do not present conceptual artwork as a real product screenshot.

## Writing style

- Be direct, specific and operational.
- Distinguish implemented, prototype, planned and unverified capabilities.
- Avoid unsupported scale, reliability, coverage or performance claims.
- Do not use emoji as a substitute for labels, icons or product information.
