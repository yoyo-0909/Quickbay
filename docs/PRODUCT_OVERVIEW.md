# QuickBay product overview

## Vision

Make independent automotive service easier to discover and easier to manage.

QuickBay is being developed as two connected experiences: a customer-facing workshop discovery and service journey, and an operations workspace for independent multi-brand workshops. Platform administration supports partner onboarding and directory governance.

## Primary users

### Vehicle owners

People who want to find a suitable workshop, request a service and understand what is happening with their vehicle.

### Workshop owners and teams

Independent garages and service centres that need a practical way to organise incoming work and day-to-day service operations.

### Platform operators

The team responsible for workshop onboarding, directory quality and platform governance.

## Product principles

- **Trust before scale:** do not inflate listing counts or claim coverage that has not been measured.
- **Source transparency:** preserve attribution and usage rights for directory records.
- **Operational clarity:** make service states understandable and meaningful.
- **Least privilege:** enforce permissions in the backend, not only in the interface.
- **Regional validation:** prove the pilot workflow before broad geographic expansion.

## Current development boundaries

The default-branch README and setup guide describe a browser demo with Supabase integration scaffolding. A live backend is not yet provisioned in that setup. Payment processing, outbound messaging and phone OTP are not part of the current enabled scope.

## Proposed measures to validate

These are future product measures, not current performance claims:

- Workshop onboarding completion and approval time.
- Booking request-to-confirmation rate.
- Job-card creation and stage-update completion.
- Directory record freshness and duplicate rate.
- Customer service-history completeness.
