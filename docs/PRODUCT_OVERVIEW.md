# QuickBay — Product overview

## Vision

Make independent automotive service easier to discover, operate and trust.

QuickBay brings two connected experiences together: a customer-facing service journey and an operations workspace for independent multi-brand workshops. Administration tools support partner onboarding, catalogue governance and platform oversight.

## Primary users

### Vehicle owners
People who need to find a suitable workshop, request a service, understand progress and keep a useful record of vehicle maintenance.

### Workshop owners and teams
Independent garages and service centres that need a practical way to organise bookings, bays, technicians and job execution without adopting a large enterprise system.

### Platform administrators
The team responsible for partner applications, directory quality, service catalogues, support workflows and platform governance.

## Core journeys

### Customer journey
1. Create an account using email and password.
2. Maintain a vehicle profile and garage.
3. Search permitted workshop listings.
4. Submit a booking request.
5. Follow the job/service status exposed by the workshop.
6. Review estimates and service records available in the account.
7. Use private support or feedback channels when needed.

### Workshop journey
1. Apply to join the partner network.
2. Receive an approved workshop membership through the controlled onboarding process.
3. Review booking requests.
4. Allocate an available bay and create a job card.
5. Move the job through its service stages.
6. Manage technicians and operational records.
7. Maintain estimates, invoice records, inventory and removed-parts handover information where the relevant workflow is enabled.

### Administration journey
1. Review workshop applications.
2. Govern partner and catalogue records.
3. Review platform activity and support queues.
4. Moderate private feedback.
5. Inspect audit events and directory provenance.

## Product principles

- **Trust over vanity metrics:** never inflate listing counts or claim coverage that has not been measured.
- **Source transparency:** retain attribution and display rights for third-party directory records.
- **Least privilege:** customer, workshop and administrator access must be separated by database policy, not just hidden UI.
- **Operational clarity:** a status should mean something concrete to the person using it.
- **Regional validation:** prove the pilot workflow in Tiruvallur and nearby localities before broad geographic claims.
- **No misleading commerce:** an invoice register is not a payment gateway.

## Scope boundaries

The current development phase intentionally excludes live payment processing, outbound messaging, phone OTP, unlicensed data imports and production promotion without explicit release approval.

## Success measures to validate

These are proposed product metrics, not current performance claims:

- Workshop application completion and approval time.
- Booking request-to-confirmation rate.
- Job-card creation and stage-update completion.
- Bay utilisation and average job turnaround.
- Directory record freshness, duplicate rate and source coverage.
- Customer repeat usage and service-history completeness.
